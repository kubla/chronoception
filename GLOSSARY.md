# Chronoception

Develop an intuitive sense of elapsed time through cued training and unaided estimation.

## Language

**Interval**: The duration a person intends to perceive, such as five minutes.

**Attempt**: One estimate of an interval, followed by the observed elapsed time and error.

**Session**: A deliberate period of practice containing cued intervals or unaided attempts. Ending a session preserves its observations.

**Training**: Learning the feel of a chosen interval through repeated cues. A cued interval is not an estimate.

**Test**: Estimating when a chosen interval has elapsed without a running clock or interval cue.

**Calibration**: How closely a person's estimates match the intended interval, considered separately for each duration over time.

**Cue**: A perceptible marker that can orient practice in time. Cue delivery and the person's estimate are separate observations.

**Quiet practice**: A deliberate silent session in which interval cues orient attention to elapsed time.

**Cue request**: A request for a device to issue an interval marker. A requested marker and a marker someone felt are different observations.
