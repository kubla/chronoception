Prepared for Michael’s personal developer membership. Not submitted. App name and bundle identifier availability remain to be established in App Store Connect.

**Name:** Chronoception

**Subtitle:** Practice your sense of time

**Description**

Choose an interval. Put the clock away. Tap when it feels complete.

Chronoception is a practice in noticing elapsed time. Use your iPhone or Apple Watch, choose a duration and make one, three or five estimates. Each attempt gets immediate feedback: how early or late you were, and the time you estimated.

Review your observations in local history. Phone and Watch work independently, with a separate history on each device.

No account or subscription is required.

**Keywords:** time,perception,meditation,interval,practice,attention,watch

**Support:** https://github.com/kubla/chronoception/issues

## Submission facts

- Individual estimation practice is free. No purchases or community features are implemented in this build.
- The app does not transmit practice observations to a developer-operated service. No third-party runtime SDKs, ads or analytics are included. Apple device backups and user-initiated support reports are separate from app collection.
- The app does not request HealthKit, notifications, microphone, location or contact access.
- Review instructions: choose an interval, Begin, tap Now at any time, inspect early/late feedback, Finish and open History. No login, external device or server is needed. The Watch app can run without the phone app.
- Use captures from the signed release candidate for store screenshots. CI development captures establish behavior and layout; they do not establish the signed release’s identity or physical haptics.
