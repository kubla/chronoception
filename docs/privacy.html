Chronoception

Your practice, stored locally.

This policy describes the native iPhone and Apple Watch app, version 0.1.0. Updated November 3, 2026.

Practice history

Intervals, attempt times and results are stored in the app’s local history. Chronoception does not send these observations to Michael Tiffany or to an analytics service. Phone and Watch histories are separate.

Accounts and tracking

The app has no account system, advertising, tracking or third-party analytics SDK. It does not request access to Apple Health, contacts, location or microphone.

Your device

Apple controls device backups and system diagnostics according to your settings. Local history may be included in a device backup. Removing the app removes its local history from that installation; manage any backups through your Apple settings.

Support

You can report a problem or ask a privacy question on GitHub. An issue is public: include only information you want to share. The app does not automatically attach your practice history.
